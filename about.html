Who Is Tony?

Tony Starr is a male stripper and party entertainer working out of Jacksonville, FL. He performs at bachelorette parties, milestone birthdays, divorce parties and girls' nights — at homes, beach rentals and hotel suites across the First Coast.

He works on his own, not through an agency. When you text, you're talking to Tony. When the doorbell rings, it's Tony. That keeps planning simple and means there's no guessing who shows up.

Every show is built around the group. Some parties want a loud, wild night; others want something playful that won't make Grandma leave early. Tell him what your crowd is like and he'll read the room from there.
